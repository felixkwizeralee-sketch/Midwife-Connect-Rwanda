Levels of Organization

Learn how the human body is organized from the smallest living unit to the complete human organism.

🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

  • Define the major levels of body organization.
  • Explain how cells form tissues.
  • Explain how tissues form organs.
  • Describe how organs work together in organ systems.
  • Recall the sequence from cell to organism.

1. Introduction

The human body is not one simple structure. It is made of many smaller structures that work together in an organized way.

These structures are arranged in levels of increasing complexity.

Cell → Tissue → Organ → Organ System → Organism

2. Cell

🔬

Cell

A cell is the basic structural and functional unit of the human body.

The human body contains many different types of cells. Examples include red blood cells, nerve cells, muscle cells and epithelial cells.

Midwifery example:

Red blood cells transport oxygen throughout the mother's and baby's body through the circulation.

3. Tissue

🧩

Tissue

Tissue is a group of similar cells that work together to perform a particular function.

The four major types of human tissue are:

  • Epithelial tissue
  • Connective tissue
  • Muscle tissue
  • Nervous tissue

4. Organ

🫀

Organ

An organ is a structure made of two or more types of tissues working together to perform specific functions.

Examples of organs include the heart, lungs, kidneys, liver and uterus.

Midwifery example:

The uterus is an organ made of several tissues. Its muscular layer, the myometrium, plays an important role in uterine contractions during labour.

5. Organ System

⚙️

Organ System

An organ system is a group of organs that work together to perform important functions.

Examples include:

  • Cardiovascular system
  • Respiratory system
  • Digestive system
  • Nervous system
  • Urinary system
  • Reproductive system

6. Organism

🧑

Organism

An organism is a complete living individual made up of interacting organ systems.

A human being is an organism. All body systems work together to maintain life and internal balance.

7. Putting It All Together

Remember the order:

Cell ↓ Tissue ↓ Organ ↓ Organ System ↓ Organism

For example, muscle cells can form muscle tissue. Different tissues form an organ such as the uterus. The uterus works with other reproductive organs as part of the reproductive system.

🧠 Exam Tip

If an examination asks for the levels of organization, remember:

Cell → Tissue → Organ → Organ System → Organism

🧠 Quick Review

1. What is the basic structural and functional unit of the body?
The cell.
2. What is a tissue?
A group of similar cells working together to perform a particular function.
3. Give two examples of organs.
Examples include the heart, lungs, kidneys, liver and uterus.
4. What is an organ system?
A group of organs working together to perform important functions.
5. Arrange these in the correct order: organ, cell, organism, tissue, organ system.
Cell → Tissue → Organ → Organ System → Organism.
